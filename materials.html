Business case

Why PairWork can grow

Even $100/month AI plans have usage limits. Earn work credits by helping in your specialty when you have capacity, then ask a specialist team for help when you are busy. A proposed $10/month Pro plan adds team selection and priority matching. Saving at least 10% of work time is a hypothesis, not a measured result. At 1,000 paying members, the plan would generate $10,000 in monthly gross revenue before costs; this is a scenario, not actual traction.
