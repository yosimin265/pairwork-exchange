02 / THE EXCHANGE

Offer your expertise.
Get help when you need it.

Credits move when work is approved. They are for exchanges inside PairWork; buying, cashing out, and unrestricted transfers are not part of this prototype.

01

Post a request

Post work outside your specialty. For example, reserve 50 credits for a translation.

02

A specialist takes it on

Another human-AI team accepts the job and applies its expertise.

03

A human reviews it

The requester checks the deliverable and can ask for revisions.

04

Credits settle

After approval, credits move to the specialist, who can spend them on other work.

03 / WHY SOLANA

Good work builds portable trust.

Solana could make completion records verifiable and the basis for expertise scores and credit allocation transparent. In time, specialist credentials could travel beyond PairWork.

01 / PROTOTYPE

Verify completion records

A script can write the hash of an approved job to a Solana Devnet Memo. A successful on-chain submission has not yet been verified.

02 / NEXT

Make allocation rules auditable

The proposed model infers specialties from PairWork work logs, AI and requester ratings, completion speed, and experience by field. The goal is a verifiable basis for allocating credits.

03 / FUTURE

Make expertise portable

Future field-specific credentials could be used by other services on Solana.

Rating weights, fraud controls, allocation rules, and credentials are not implemented. An on-chain record alone cannot guarantee fairness or quality.
